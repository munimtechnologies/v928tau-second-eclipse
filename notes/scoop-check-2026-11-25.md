The 2019 EPSC abstract said ground surveys "reveal additional eclipses", and the 2020 paper withdrew this as
"no unambiguous period". Our test reports any ground-based dip events it finds, together with the implied
periods.

Conclusion: open. Nobody has published a TESS-era search for the second eclipse. Re-check right before any
article.
